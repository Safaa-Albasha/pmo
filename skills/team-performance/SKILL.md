# Team performance / أداء الفريق

## Signals to review
- Delivery: velocity stability (agile), activity completion vs plan, rework.
- Load: over-allocation (resource plan), overtime, single points of failure (one person on several critical activities).
- Health: quick pulse survey (1–5) on clarity, workload, collaboration, psychological safety, support; trend over time.
- Impediments: open items in `issues.json` older than one week.

## Actions
- Remove impediments (escalate if outside team control).
- Rebalance work using float before adding people.
- Clarify roles (RACI) and decision rights; empower the team to decide within agreed limits.
- Recognise contributions in status reports.
- Development: skills gaps → training or pairing.

## Conflict
Prefer collaborate/problem-solve; compromise when time-bound; escalate only after direct resolution fails. Record agreements as decisions or actions.

Record retro/health actions in `lessons.json` with an owner and due date. Do not record personal performance judgements about named individuals in project files.
